About me

I'm a 400-level MBBS student at the University of Lagos. Medicine is where I started, but I want to do more than practise it. I want to understand the body the way a physicist understands a system: from first principles, with mathematics and data.

So alongside my clinical training I'm teaching myself the quantitative side of biomedical science: maths, physics, data science, machine learning, systems biology and computational neuroscience. I build projects to learn, and I explain science online so others can learn with me.

  • Based inLagos, Nigeria

In progress

MeDIS

A biomedical knowledge graph and mathematical modelling platform. The aim is to map how biological entities and processes connect, then use that map to run simulations and find what we haven't thought of yet.

  • Alternative routes to the same biological outcome
  • Mechanisms behind currently incurable diseases
  • Drug repurposing
Starting now

Malaria Hunter

My first machine-learning project: an image classifier that learns to spot malaria parasites in blood-smear images. It's my way into ML, and a step toward a long-term goal of building an AI research assistant for biology.

Science communication

I make science explainers for TikTok and Instagram, and I've just started an astronomy and astrophysics channel. It has carousels, short videos, a word of the day and worked astrophysics problems, following a 10-part curriculum from the basics of astronomy to the frontiers of physics.
